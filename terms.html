Your package includes only what is listed in your proposal. Anything else — extra pages, new features, a different design direction — is extra work. We always quote extra work in writing and start it only after you approve the quote.

3. Price and payment

We always send an invoice first. A 50% advance is payable on confirmation; the balance is due before the final website goes live on your domain. Maintenance is billed monthly in advance. Clients in India pay by UPI; international clients pay via Payoneer, in the currency on the invoice. Prices quoted are firm for the validity period printed on the proposal.

4. Timeline and our delivery guarantee

We deliver the built website within 5–7 working days of receiving (a) your complete content and (b) your advance, whichever is later. If content or feedback from your side is delayed, the delivery date moves by the same number of days, and we confirm the new date in writing.

If we are late and the delay is solely our fault: if you have taken maintenance, your first month of maintenance is free; if you have not taken maintenance, we will instead do one round of content updates for you (menu, photos, timings or offers) free of charge.

5. Your content

You provide the menu, photos, text and logo. You confirm you own them or have permission to use them. Your content always remains yours.

Every package includes 2 rounds of revisions. A "round" means one consolidated list of changes sent at one time. Please send revision requests within 7 days of each delivery so the project keeps moving.

Your domain name and hosting are bought and held in your own name and accounts, and always remain your property. You pay the registrar/hosting charges directly. We only take access to set things up and launch your site.

9. Maintenance (if you take it)

Maintenance covers software updates, regular backups, and small content changes — menu prices, photos, timings, offers (2 small updates a month included). It does not cover redesigns, new pages or new features; those are quoted separately. It is month-to-month: you can stop anytime before the next month begins, and nothing further is charged.

Either of us can cancel in writing. If we have not yet started work, your advance is refunded in full. Once work has started, the advance is not refundable — it covers the work already done — but you owe nothing further, except for any extra work you approved and we completed. If we are unable to deliver because of a fault on our side, we refund everything you have paid us for the undelivered work.
