Privacy Policy

The short version: we collect only what we need to reply to your enquiry and build your website. We never sell your details, and we never share them for marketing. Last updated: 10 November 2026.

1. What we collect

When you send our enquiry form, we receive your name, your restaurant's name, your work email, your contact number (only if you choose to give it), your restaurant type and the message you write. If you email us directly, we receive the details in that email. That is all — this website has no accounts, no sign-ups and no payment forms.

2. How we use it

Only for your enquiry and your project: to reply to you, build your free demo, prepare your proposal and invoice, build and maintain your website, and keep a basic record of our work together. We do not use your details for unrelated marketing, and we do not add you to any mailing list.

3. What we never do

We never sell your personal details. We never share them with third parties for their marketing. Your contact number, if you give it, is used only to coordinate your own project — never for sales calls.

4. Services that process data for us

Our form is delivered by FormSubmit, our email runs on Gmail (Google), and this website is served by our hosting provider. These services process your details only to deliver the form, the email and the page to us. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your browser's standard request data (such as IP address) to serve the font files.

5. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores one small preference on your own device — which version of the site (India or Worldwide) you last viewed — so the right page opens next time. It stays on your device and is not sent to us.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and project records for as long as they are needed for our work together and for normal business records (invoices and agreements). If you ask us to delete your enquiry details, we will — unless we must keep a record for legal or accounting reasons, in which case we keep only that record.

7. Your rights

Your details are yours. At any time you can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello.tablefly@gmail.com and we will act on your request promptly.

8. Who this site is for

Tablefly Studio serves businesses. This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect details from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. See also our Terms & Conditions.
